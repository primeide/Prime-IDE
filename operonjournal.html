Operon Journal — Academic Journal Publishing Platform

Operon Journal is a peer-reviewed academic publishing platform we helped modernize for better editorial workflows, submissions management and discoverability. The platform focuses on providing a reliable submission-to-publication pipeline and improving visibility for authors and reviewers.

Project Overview

PrimeIDE delivered a secure, accessible and SEO-optimized journal system with features tailored for academic publishers:

  • Submission management and multi-stage peer review
  • Author & reviewer dashboards and notifications
  • Article metadata, DOI-ready exports and citation support
  • Advanced search and indexing for discoverability
  • Responsive, accessible design (WCAG-friendly)
  • Secure admin area, role-based access and backups

Technologies & Approach

  • Custom CMS integrations (PHP / MySQL)
  • Search engine optimization and semantic markup
  • Secure publishing workflow and automated backups
Live site

Outcome

The delivered platform streamlined editorial operations, reduced peer-review turnaround time and improved article discoverability across academic indexes.
